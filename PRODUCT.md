# Product context

Local desktop building editor for beginners who need direct 3D modeling, reversible edits, reliable portable project files and GLB with independently playable doors. Windows and macOS, English and Simplified Chinese. Compact editor toolbar, floor list, viewport and contextual inspector. Simple operation and geometry/data correctness precede polish. All product requirements are in docs/PRD-v0.3.md. No marketing layout or decoration. Architectural and color boundaries in AGENTS.md are binding.
